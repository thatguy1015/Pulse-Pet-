Meet PulsePet

A small digital companion designed to remember what you tell it and respond in a more natural, personalised way.

Persistent Memory

PulsePet can store and recall simple user preferences such as likes and favourite colours.

AI Conversation

General messages can be handled using AI-generated responses for more flexible interaction.

Future Embodiment

The project can later be extended into VR or robotic forms using the same companion concept.
